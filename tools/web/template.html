Kinlings preview

Two badges running the game. Yours is on the left (or top). The other belongs to a friend standing next to you, so you can play both sides of a swap.



  

    
A quick tour

    
    
      
  1. Press SELECT five times on each badge to hatch its egg.
    2. 
      
  2. Tap Someone walks past a few times. Each passer-by is a badge without the app; within a few seconds both creatures forage a snack from them.
    3. 
      
  3. On your badge press SELECT, pick @abcd_friend and press SELECT again. On the friend's badge press SELECT to say yes.
    4. 
      
  4. Your creature has now been fed by one person, the friend is in your pack, and because they are an ABCD member it is wearing their crown. LEFT and RIGHT take it off and on.
    5. 
      
  5. Swap again and nothing grows: only a new person's first snack counts. Untick the box above and start over to see a swap with a non-member.
    6. 
      
  6. Skip 4 hours puts an unfed creature to sleep. UP shows your pack, DOWN the help screen, CANCEL goes back.
    7. 
    

  


  
This page runs the game's real code, but on a stand-in for the badge: a browser Lua and a screen drawn to look like the badge's. Fonts and exact positions differ, and the badge's memory limit is not enforced here. On a keyboard, the arrow keys, Enter and Escape drive your badge.
